## Lodging Budget

Based on 11–12 respondents who provided budget figures (per bedroom, per day):

| Statistic | Ideal Budget | Maximum Budget |
|---|---|---|
| Mean | $211 | $304 |
| Median | $200 | $275 |
| Min | $75 | $150 |
| Max | $400 | $550 |
| 25th Percentile | $150 | $200 |
| 75th Percentile | $250 | $363 |
| Std Dev | $101 | $130 |

### Key Takeaways — Budget

- **Target range:** Aim for $150–$250/night per bedroom to satisfy most respondents' ideal budgets.
- **Hard ceiling:** Stay under $350/night per bedroom to remain within most people's maximum.
- **Wide spread:** Budgets vary significantly ($75–$400 ideal, $150–$550 max), indicating a need to offer range or tiered options.

## Lodging Type Preferences

Respondents selected all acceptable lodging types (multi-select). 27 responses.

| Lodging Type | Selections | % of Respondents |
|---|---|---|
| Hybrid Cabins + Condos Area | 26 | 96% |
| Cabins | 22 | 81% |
| Hotel | 9 | 33% |

### Key Takeaways — Lodging Type

- **Hybrid is near-universal:** 26 of 27 respondents selected the hybrid cabins + condos option, making it the clear preferred format.
- **Cabins are strongly favored:** 81% want cabins available, with multiple respondents noting the extra space for gathering is a plus.
- **Hotel is supplementary:** Only a third of respondents selected hotel, and those who did almost always selected it alongside cabins and hybrid. No one selected hotel alone.
- **Recommendation:** Prioritize a **hybrid resort with cabins/condos and optional hotel-style rooms** to satisfy the widest range of preferences.

## Summary & Recommendations

1. **Location:** Focus the search on the **Rockies, Northeast, and West Coast** — these regions have the strongest support and least resistance. Avoid Texas and the Southeast.
2. **Venue type:** Prioritize properties with **kitchens in units, a walkable layout, reliable wifi/cell, A/C, a common room, and proximity to a grocery store**.
3. **Budget:** Target **$150–$250/night per bedroom** for ideal satisfaction; do not exceed **$350/night**.
4. **Best-positioned states:** **CO, ID, KY, ME, MN, OH, OR, PA, TX, and WI** have nominations, investigation interest, and volunteer coordinators (coordinators can also investigate). Within the preferred regions, **OR** (West Coast) and **CO** (Rockies) align best with regional preferences.
5. **Activities:** Ensure access to **swimming, picnic areas, hiking, and national parks**. A banquet area and laundry facilities are also valued.
